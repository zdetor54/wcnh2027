Choose your optional add-ons now. They are purchased together with registration and form part of your total. All add-on prices are for the registered attendee only; add-ons are not sold separately on this page.

Gala dinner

Join us for the conference gala dinner at Κτήμα Παυλάκη (Ktima Pavlaki), including the prize awards.

Non-delegate gala tickets: €100 per person, €50 for children under 14, and free for children younger than 4 years.

Airport transfers

€27 per person, per trip between the hotel and Herakleion or Chania airport. Select trips for the registered attendee only.
