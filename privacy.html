Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ARK4ART LTD, on behalf of the organisers of the World Congress on Neurohypophysial Hormones 2027 (WCNH 2027, "we", "us" or "our"), collects, use and protect your personal information when you use this website and register for or take part in the congress.

1. What data we collect

  • Personal information: name, email address, affiliation, registration category, and other details you provide when registering, submitting an abstract, expressing interest or contacting us.
  • Payment information: registration selections and payment status. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card details. For bank transfers, we receive the payer name and payment reference from our bank.
  • Abstract submissions: abstract content, author names and affiliations sent to us by email.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser information that our hosting and content-delivery providers process automatically when you load the website.

2. How we use your data

  • To process registrations, payments and abstract submissions, and to manage participation in the congress.
  • To communicate with you about the congress or your queries.
  • To prepare the congress programme and abstract materials.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data to perform our agreement with you when you register, on the basis of your consent, for our legitimate interests in organising the congress, or to comply with legal requirements.

4. Data sharing

Your data may be shared with the WCNH 2027 organising committee and with trusted service providers solely for the purposes described above. These include:

  • Stripe: card payment processing.
  • Wise: bank transfer payments.
  • Google: email (Gmail), interest forms (Google Forms) and web fonts.
  • GitHub, Cloudflare and jsDelivr: website hosting, payment checkout handling and content delivery.

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing purposes. If you choose to join the optional WhatsApp group for room sharing, the information you post there is visible to other members and is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

5. Data storage and security

We store your data securely and retain it only as long as necessary for the purposes stated, or as required by law. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at wcnh2027@gmail.com.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Third-party services we link to, such as Stripe's checkout page and Google Forms, may set their own cookies under their own policies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact:
The WCNH 2027 Organising Committee
