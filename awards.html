WCNH 2027 Glenn I. Hatton Award Competition

We are delighted to invite PhD students and postdoctoral researchers to participate in the WCNH 2027 Glenn I. Hatton Award Competition, celebrating outstanding scientific contributions by early-career researchers in the field of neurohypophysial hormones.

Established through a generous endowment from Professor Glenn I. Hatton (opens in a new tab), a distinguished neuroscientist at the University of California, Riverside, the award aims to encourage, recognise and reward scientific excellence among the next generation of researchers.

Professor Hatton made pioneering contributions to our understanding of the neurohypophysial system. To learn more about his remarkable scientific legacy, we encourage you to read the excellent tribute by Professor Gareth Leng and colleagues (opens in a new tab).

WCNH 2027 will present four Glenn I. Hatton Awards, recognising two outstanding PhD students and two exceptional postdoctoral researchers.

Each recipient will receive:

  • €1,000 monetary award
  • Certificate of excellence
  • Complimentary admission to the Conference Gala Dinner
  • The opportunity to present their research in a short oral presentation at WCNH 2027

The awards will be formally presented during the Congress.

Eligibility

The competition is open to:

  1. PhD students currently enrolled in a doctoral programme.
  2. Postdoctoral researchers within five years of their PhD award at the time of application.

The following conditions apply:

  • The research submitted must not have been previously published, including as a conference abstract, or presented at a national or international scientific meeting. Presentations at institutional, regional or state-level meetings are permitted.
  • Previous recipients of a Glenn I. Hatton Award are not eligible to enter the competition again.

How to Apply

PhD students:

  1. Register for WCNH 2027
  2. Submit an abstract and the following documentation:
    • A letter of support of up to 500 words from your PhD mentor.
    • Your current academic CV.

Combine the abstract, the letter of support and CV into a single PDF document and email it to:

wcnh2027@gmail.com

Email subject: WCNH2027_Abstract_YOURSURNAME_Hatton_PhD

Postdoctoral researchers:

  1. Register for WCNH 2027
  2. Submit an abstract and the following documentation:
    • Two letters of support (up to 500 words each): One from your mentor, and one from an established scientist in the field.
    • Your current academic CV.

Combine the abstract, the letters of support and CV into a single PDF document and email it to:

wcnh2027@gmail.com

Email subject: WCNH2027_Abstract_YOURSURNAME_Hatton_Postdoc

Application deadline for both awards: 15 January 2027. Both the online abstract submission and supporting documentation must be received by the deadline for an application to be considered.

Evaluation Criteria

Applications will be evaluated by the WCNH 2027 Organising Committee based on their scientific merit and overall research excellence.

WCNH 2027 Travel awards

We may be able to offer a small number of travel awards to support early-career researchers (PhD students or postdocs) who need financial help to attend WCNH 2027.

If you would like to be considered for a travel award, please register by the Early Bird Deadline (15th January 2027) and submit an abstract, together with your CV and a motivation letter (up to 500 words). Your motivation letter should provide a justification for your request for a travel award, and your motivation for attending WCNH 2027.

We will aim to notify successful applicants by the first week of February 2027.
